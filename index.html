Meet David

I got into financial coaching because I needed it myself.

David Burton · Financial Coach

My wife and I weren’t always on the same page when it came to managing money. I had built a detailed spreadsheet, tracked everything, and genuinely enjoyed digging into the numbers. For me, it worked. For her, it was overwhelming.

We eventually worked with a financial coach who helped us simplify what we were doing. My wife wasn’t overwhelmed by all the information anymore, and I discovered that I didn’t actually need all of that complexity either. We found a simpler way to manage and track our money that worked better for both of us.

That experience changed the way I thought about personal finance. A good financial plan isn’t just about finding the “right” system on paper. It has to work for the actual people using it.

I’ve always enjoyed working with numbers, solving problems, and finding ways to make things more efficient. I also genuinely enjoy teaching and helping people develop new skills. Financial coaching brought those things together in a way I hadn’t realized was possible.

Today, that’s a big part of how I approach coaching: understand how you think, what matters to you, and what you’re trying to accomplish—then help you build an approach that actually works for your life.
